Booking Information

Everything you need to know before confirming your journey with Viatrix Tours & Travel.

01

Payment Methods

  • Mobile Money and bank transfer
  • Visa or Mastercard where available
  • PayPal where available
  • Cash on arrival only where agreed in advance
02

Deposits & Balance

  • Your quotation or invoice states the required booking deposit.
  • The remaining balance and due date appear on your invoice.
  • Official invoices and payment confirmations or receipts are provided.
03

Cancellation & Refunds

  • Send cancellation requests through an official Viatrix communication channel.
  • Charges and eligible refunds depend on timing, confirmed services and supplier terms.
  • Some permits or supplier payments may be non-refundable.
  • Rescheduling may be offered where applicable.
04

Travel Information

  • Travellers are responsible for passports, visas and entry requirements.
  • Comprehensive travel insurance is strongly recommended.
  • Health, fitness and age requirements may apply to certain activities.
  • Park, permit and activity instructions must be followed.
