Privacy Policy

This page explains how Viatrix Tours & Travel Co. Ltd. handles personal information supplied through enquiries, booking requests and travel arrangements.

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Viatrix uses traveller information to answer enquiries, prepare itineraries, arrange requested services, process bookings and support journeys. We aim to collect only information reasonably needed for those purposes.

Information we collect

Depending on the enquiry or booking, information may include names, contact details, travel dates, destination preferences, passport details where required, dietary needs, accessibility or health information voluntarily supplied for travel planning, and booking or payment records.

How information is used

  • To respond to enquiries and create quotations or itineraries.
  • To confirm and manage bookings, permits, accommodation, transport and activities.
  • To communicate important service, safety or itinerary information.
  • To maintain booking, financial and customer-service records.
  • To send marketing updates only where the recipient has agreed or where otherwise permitted by applicable law, with an option to opt out.

Sharing and service providers

Viatrix may share relevant information with hotels, transport operators, guides, airlines, park authorities, permit issuers, activity providers, payment services or other suppliers needed to deliver the requested journey. Information is not sold. Suppliers may process information under their own privacy terms and applicable law.

Storage and security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect booking information. No internet or storage system is completely secure, so travellers should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through unverified channels and should confirm unexpected requests directly with Viatrix.

Retention and your choices

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the booking, customer support, record-keeping and applicable legal or accounting obligations. You may ask Viatrix to correct your contact information, stop optional marketing messages, or explain the information held about your enquiry or booking, subject to applicable law and necessary business records.
